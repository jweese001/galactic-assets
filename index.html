Galactic Assets

Procedural generation demos for the Khora Engine

Khora Engine is a procedural space exploration game where players choose one of two paths: Architect or Astronaut. Generate entire galaxies from a seed. Craft and deploy unique sectors filled with explorable star systems—or venture into the unknown to scan, mine, colonize, and defend your claim on the frontier.

These demos are the rendering foundation: real-time procedural shaders for planets, stars, and galaxies built with Three.js and custom GLSL. Work in progress.
